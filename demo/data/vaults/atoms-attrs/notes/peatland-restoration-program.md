Since its launch in 2019, the Peatland and Mangrove Restoration Agency program has dramatically reduced fire risk and equipped residents of 121 villages in coastal West Kalimantan with new skills and resources. The BRGM, with support from UN Office for Project Services and the Ministry of Forestry, has conducted restoration projects across 852 villages in Kalimantan, Papua, and Sumatra.
