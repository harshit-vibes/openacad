The Whitaker Peace and Development Initiative (WPDI) operates the Youth Peacemaker Network to mentor and support youth in creating coalitions of peacebuilders and entrepreneurs globally. The program provides rigorous training in conflict resolution, particularly targeting former child soldiers and conflict-affected youth, such as those from refugee settlements.
