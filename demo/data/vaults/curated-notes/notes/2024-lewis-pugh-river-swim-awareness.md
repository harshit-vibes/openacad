Lewis Pugh, the first appointed UNEP Patron of the Oceans and an English-South African long-distance swimmer, conducted a swim down the length of the Hudson River starting August 13, 2023, timed to arrive in New York City during the UN 78th General Assembly to draw attention to the critical importance of healthy river ecosystems and environmental progress in ecosystem recovery.
