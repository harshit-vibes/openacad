Farmers in the Indonesian province of West Kalimantan are adapting their agricultural techniques with a more climate-friendly approach to farming, without burning land. This adaptation is part of an initiative by Indonesia’s Peat and Mangrove Restoration Agency (BRGM), which has led to a 50% increase in farmers' incomes and a healthier peatland that reduces greenhouse gas emissions.
