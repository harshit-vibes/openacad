The SDGs are the universally-agreed road map to bridge economic and geopolitical divides, restore trust and rebuilt solidarity. Failure to make progress means inequalities will continue to deepen, increasing the risk of a fragmented, two-speed world. No country can afford to see the 2030 Agenda fail.
