UNODC partnered with Malawi's government (Ministry of Homeland Security) and Sweden to conduct training courses for truck drivers to reduce human trafficking and migrant smuggling. The curriculum educates drivers about legal penalties (imprisonment up to 14 years, loss of truck/employment, criminal record) and links trafficking to broader illicit activities including drug and firearms smuggling, exploitation, abuse, and violence.
