Rajab Emad, a former child soldier in the Sudan People's Liberation Army (SPLA) for three years, was reintegrated in the Kiryandongo Refugee Settlement in Uganda. With WPDI mentorship and conflict resolution training, he transitioned to becoming a youth peacemaker and leader organizing community activities such as football-based peace programs.
